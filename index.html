About Us

More Than Just a Gym

At FitForge, we believe fitness is a lifestyle, not a destination. Our mission is to empower every member to unlock their full potential through expert coaching, world-class facilities, and a community that pushes you to be your best. From your first session to your hundredth PR, we are with you every rep of the way.

Our 12,000 sq ft facility features the latest strength and cardio equipment, dedicated functional training zones, a private yoga studio, and recovery amenities. Every program is designed by certified professionals who tailor training to your goals, your body, and your schedule.

All plans can be billed monthly or yearly with a 20% discount on annual billing.

Absolutely. We welcome all fitness levels. Every new member gets a free fitness assessment, and our trainers will help you start at the right level. We have beginner-friendly classes and programs designed specifically for those new to fitness.

Group classes are included with the Pro and Elite memberships. Basic members can attend classes for a drop-in fee. We offer over 50 classes per week including HIIT, Yoga, CrossFit, Zumba, Boxing, and Strength Training.
